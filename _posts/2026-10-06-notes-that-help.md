A useful note helps your future self understand what mattered. Saving the source is only the beginning.

## Capture the reason

Alongside a link or quote, add one sentence about why you saved it. That sentence is often the most useful part when you return weeks later.

## Use a small structure

Try three prompts:

- **Idea:** What is the point worth remembering?
- **Context:** Where might it be useful?
- **Next step:** Is there anything to try?

## Revisit before reorganizing

A collection grows more useful when you use it. Read a few old notes before spending an afternoon inventing new categories.
