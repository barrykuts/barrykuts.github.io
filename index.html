Barry Kuts

I’m a product builder interested in giving people more control over how they use technology.

Born and raised in NYC, I became fascinated with financial markets at 13. I studied accounting at Baruch College, earned my CPA, and began my career in tax before moving into real estate private equity. A self-taught engineer, I later led product and research strategy at Dymension, a settlement blockchain.

Outside of work I enjoy baking, practicing brazilian jiu-jitsu and spending time with family, friends, and my puppy Bugsy.
